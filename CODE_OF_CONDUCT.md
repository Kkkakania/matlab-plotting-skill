# Code of Conduct

Be direct, kind, and specific.

Good discussion here should focus on plots, data shapes, MATLAB behavior,
accessibility, and maintainability. Assume contributors are trying to help, but
do not ask maintainers to accept unclear provenance, copied code, private data,
or unverifiable assets.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues and pull requests that include
personal attacks, spam, private information, or material that cannot be safely
redistributed.
